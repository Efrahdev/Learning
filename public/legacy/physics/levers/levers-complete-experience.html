💡 The Trade-Off

Levers don't create energy — they transform it. Less force means more distance. More force means less distance. The work is always the same!

⚡ Mechanical Advantage

MA = Effort Arm ÷ Load Arm

MA > 1 = Force multiplier (easier to lift) | MA < 1 = Speed multiplier (faster movement)
