Category 1

Part–Whole

Skill focus: reading a fraction as a part of a fixed whole, and recovering the whole from a stated part.

Foundation
Q1

A chocolate bar is split into 12 equal squares. Priya eats 5 squares and her brother eats 3. What fraction of the bar is left? Simplify your answer.

Q2

In a class of 28 students, 3/7 wear glasses. How many students do not wear glasses?

Challenge
Q3

A ribbon is cut into equal pieces. Seven of those pieces together make up 7/10 of the ribbon. How many equal pieces make the whole ribbon, and what fraction is one piece?

Q4

In a bag of marbles, 1/4 are red, 1/3 are blue, and the remaining 20 are green. How many marbles are in the bag?

Achiever
Q5

A wall is painted in three sessions. In the first, 1/3 of the wall is painted; in the second, 1/4 of the whole wall is painted; in the third, the painter finishes the remaining 25 m². Find the total area of the wall.

Q6

A pile of coins is shared so that the first person gets 1/2 of the total, the second gets 1/3 of the total, and the third gets the rest. If the third person’s share is 8 coins fewer than the first person’s, how many coins are there in total?

Fraction of a Fraction

Foundation
Q7

Find 2/3 of 3/4. Give your answer in lowest terms.

Q8

Challenge
Q9

In a garden, 5/8 of the plot is lawn. Of the lawn, 2/5 lies in shade. Of the shaded lawn, 3/4 is damp. What fraction of the whole garden is damp, shaded lawn?

Q10

Half of a cake is left. A guest takes 2/3 of what is left; then a child takes 1/4 of what now remains. What fraction of the whole cake is left at the end?

Achiever
Q11

A tank of water is emptied in stages. Each stage removes 1/3 of the water currently in the tank. After three stages, what fraction of the original water remains?

Q12

Of a group of people, 3/5 are adults. Of the adults, 5/6 hold a driving licence. Of those with a licence, 4/9 drove to the event. If 40 licensed adults drove, how many people are in the group?

Q18
