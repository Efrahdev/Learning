01 Mirror Images Apex

Figure (X) is first rotated 90° clockwise about its centre; the result is then viewed in a plane mirror M placed vertically to its right. Which option shows the final image?

B

Answer C

Reasoning

Rotate 90° CW: the flag-pole turns horizontal with the pennant hanging on the right end and the shaded square moving to the top. Then reflect across the vertical mirror: left and right swap while up/down is preserved. Both steps must be executed in order — the two operations do not commute with a shortcut.

Answer D

Reasoning

A
B
C
D
Show answer

Reasoning

Which set of the pieces below can be assembled — with no gaps and no overlaps — into the 6 × 6 square shown? (Note: EVERY option below totals an area of exactly 36, so area alone will not decide it.)

A
A, B and E
B
A, B and C
C
A and D
D
B, C and D
Show answer

Answer B

Reasoning

Every option has area 36 — that is the trap, and it is why area is a necessary but never a sufficient test. The real check is fit. Piece A (3 × 6) covers exactly the lower half of the square. The leftover region is another 3 × 6 rectangle, and B and C are precisely the two right triangles you get by cutting that rectangle along its diagonal (legs 6 and 3). Reasoning
