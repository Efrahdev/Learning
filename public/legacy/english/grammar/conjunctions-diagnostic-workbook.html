Diagnostic instrument · not a worksheet

A conjunction is a hinge. Everything depends on how it swings.

Two ideas can be nailed together in a dozen ways, and each way says something different about how the world works. Because claims a cause. Although admits a cost. Or may or may not be a real choice. This workbook does not ask what a conjunction is called; it asks what happens to the meaning when you change one.

[ she practised nightly ]  — yet —  [ she lost the final ]   ≠   [ she practised nightly ]  — so —  [ she lost the final ]
