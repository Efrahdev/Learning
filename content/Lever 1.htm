🔬 The Three Families of Levers

Every lever belongs to one of three "classes". Click each to see how they work!

📐 Class 1

Fulcrum in the Middle

✂️ Scissors, ⚖️ Seesaw, 🔨 Hammer (pulling nails)

MA can be any value

🛒 Class 2

Load in the Middle

🛒 Wheelbarrow, 🍾 Bottle opener, 🥜 Nutcracker

MA always > 1 (Force multiplier!)

🎣 Class 3

Effort in the Middle

🎣 Fishing rod, ⚾ Baseball bat, 💪 Your arm!

MA always < 1 (Speed multiplier!)

Remember: The class is determined by what's in the MIDDLE!
